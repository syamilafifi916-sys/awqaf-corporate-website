# AWQAF Reports v1

**Tag:** `reports-v1`

Annual Reports & Audited Financial Statements of AWQAF Holdings Berhad — original PDF
documents, unmodified. These assets are consumed by the AWQAF Corporate Website
(`awqaf.my`) Transparency Centre via `REPORTS_BASE_URL`; the download links on the site
point directly at this release. Files are hosted here (GitHub Releases) because several
exceed Cloudflare Pages' 25 MB per-file limit.

- **Contents:** 20 PDFs — 10 Annual Reports (2015–2024) + 10 Audited Financial Statements (2015–2024).
- **Integrity:** original files, no compression, no modification, original filenames.
- **Total size:** ~460 MB. Largest: `Annual-Report-AWQAF-2017.pdf` (~158 MiB).
