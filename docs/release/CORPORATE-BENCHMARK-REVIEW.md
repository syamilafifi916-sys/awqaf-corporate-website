# AWQAF Corporate Website — Benchmark Review

Date: **16 July 2026**. This is a **review only** — not a mandate to redesign. AWQAF is a
community-based Corporate Waqf institution of modest scale; several benchmark practices suit much
larger sovereign funds or global charities and should **not** be imitated wholesale.

Benchmarks: Khazanah Nasional, KWSP/EPF, Temasek, Yayasan Hasanah, Wellcome Trust.

---

## Khazanah Nasional (sovereign strategic investor)
- **Already matches:** calm institutional tone; portfolio-by-sector structure; annual-report
  prominence; clear governance/board section.
- **Lacks:** an investment-thesis / strategy narrative; portfolio performance dashboards.
- **Improve after launch:** a concise "pendekatan pelaburan" statement per portfolio once verified.
- **Do not imitate:** heavy macro-economic reporting and a large media/press machine — disproportionate
  to AWQAF's scale and would read as overreach.

## KWSP / EPF (member-servicing institution)
- **Already matches:** a clear, separated member portal entry (Portal Pewakaf) distinct from public
  contribution; formal BM; downloads hub.
- **Lacks:** self-service calculators, FAQs, and multi-channel support content.
- **Improve after launch:** a short waqf FAQ and a clearer "kaedah berwakaf" walkthrough.
- **Do not imitate:** the dense transactional dashboard and account-heavy IA — AWQAF's public site
  should stay institutional, with transactions behind the portal.

## Temasek (global investor, design-forward)
- **Already matches:** editorial hierarchy, generous whitespace, restrained palette.
- **Lacks:** rich original photography, data-visualisation storytelling, sustainability reporting.
- **Improve after launch:** commission real institutional photography to replace placeholders.
- **Do not imitate:** high-production motion/video and interactive data essays — cost and maintenance
  outweigh benefit at AWQAF's scale.

## Yayasan Hasanah (closest peer — impact/community foundation)
- **Already matches:** programme-by-theme structure; welfare programmes separated from investment
  portfolios; grounded, non-salesy tone.
- **Lacks:** impact stories and outcome metrics per programme (with beneficiaries, where verifiable).
- **Improve after launch:** add *verified* impact figures/case notes to ZuriatCARE/EduWAQF/AWQAF4Health.
- **Do not imitate:** grant-application portals and CSR-partner microsites unless AWQAF runs such
  programmes.

## Wellcome Trust (endowment + mission publishing)
- **Already matches:** transparency centre, audited financials, clear mission framing.
- **Lacks:** a research/thought-leadership library (AWQAF's would be on Corporate Waqf).
- **Improve after launch:** a small "penyelidikan & penerbitan" section anchored by the founder's
  book and Corporate Waqf material — but only with cleared rights.
- **Do not imitate:** a large open-access publishing operation and global grant infrastructure.

---

## Cross-cutting takeaways
1. **Biggest genuine gap = original media + verified impact/performance data**, not structure or design.
2. AWQAF already matches peers on **IA clarity, governance transparency and tone**.
3. Stay institution-first: resist sovereign-fund breadth and charity-campaign intensity — both misfit
   AWQAF's role.

## Launch-critical defects found by this review
**None.** All gaps are post-launch enhancements dependent on content, media or verified data.
